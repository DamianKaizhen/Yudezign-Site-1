## 2. Research for "Priority 1: Location Pages"

**Objective:** Differentiate generic "service area" pages by addressing specific local pain points and housing stock characteristics.

### A. The "Houston Humidity" Factor (Critical for All Locations)
*Context:* Houston averages 75-90% humidity. This is the single biggest technical challenge for cabinetry in this region.
* **Topic to Discuss:** **Wood Movement & Material Selection.**
    *   **Fact:** Solid wood expands and contracts with humidity changes. In Houston, a 5-piece solid wood door can swell enough to rub against adjacent doors if not properly spaced.[1]
    *   **Recommendation:** Advocate for **Engineered Cores (MDF/Plywood)** for the cabinet boxes and center panels of doors. Explain that high-grade MDF is *superior* to solid wood for painted cabinets in Houston because it is dimensionally stable and will not crack at the joints like solid wood does during humidity swings.[2][1]
*   **Actionable Tip:** Explicitly mention "Moisture-Resistant (MR) Cores" for sink bases and bathroom vanities.

### B. Neighborhood-Specific Housing Angles
*   **River Oaks / West University / Memorial:**
    *   *Home Style:* Historic revivals, luxury traditional, and high-modern custom builds.
    *   *Talking Point:* **"Resale Value & Timelessness."** In these zip codes, kitchens are the primary value driver. Discuss "Inset Cabinetry" (luxury look) vs. "European Frameless" (modern luxury). Focus on high-end customizations like walnut interiors and integrated lighting.[3][4]
*   **Katy / Cypress / The Woodlands:**
    *   *Home Style:* Large-scale new construction, master-planned communities.
    *   *Talking Point:* **"The Heart of the Family Home."** These homes often have massive open-concept "Great Rooms." Focus on oversized islands (10ft+), durable finishes for families (Laminate/Acrylic), and "hidden pantries" which are trending in new builds.[3]
*   **Houston (Inner Loop/Heights):**
    *   *Home Style:* Bungalow renovations and townhomes.
    *   *Talking Point:* **"Space Maximization."** Townhomes have narrow footprints. Push **Frameless Cabinets** here specifically because they offer 10-15% more usable drawer space than framed cabinets, crucial for smaller urban kitchens.[5][6]

## 3. Research for "Priority 2: Service Pages"

**Objective:** Demonstrate technical expertise to justify custom pricing.

### A. `/services/kitchen-cabinets` (The Flagship Page)
*   **Topic: The "Work Triangle" vs. "Work Zones."**
    *   *Insight:* Modern design has moved beyond the "triangle" (sink-fridge-stove) to "zones" (Prep, Cook, Clean, Consumables).
    *   *YuDezign Value:* Explain how custom cabinetry is the *only* way to perfectly engineer these zones compared to stock cabinets which force you into 3-inch incremental sizes.[7]
*   **Topic: Frameless Construction (The "European" Angle).**
    *   *Definition:* No face frame. The door is attached directly to the box.
    *   *Benefit:* sleek lines, full access to the interior (no center stile blocking the way), and wider drawers.[8][9][5]
    *   *Citation Data:* Frameless cabinets are the standard in Europe and are rapidly gaining market share in the US for modern/transitional designs.[10]

### B. `/services/closet-systems`
*   **Topic: Floor-Based vs. Wall-Hung Systems.**
    *   *Research:* Wall-hung is cheaper/easier to clean underneath; Floor-based looks like built-in furniture and supports more weight.
    *   *Houston Angle:* Mention **"Seasonal Rotation."** Houstonians don't have heavy winter coats, but we have high AC usage. designing for airflow in closets prevents mold/mildew on leather goods.[1]

### C. `/services/garage-cabinets`
*   **Topic: Off-the-Floor Design.**
    *   *Reasoning:* Houston garages are prone to flooding and pests.
    *   *Value:* YuDezign installs "floating" garage cabinets (6 inches off the floor) to allow for easy hosing down of the garage floor and preventing water damage to the cabinet legs.[2]

## 4. Research for "Priority 3: Blog/Resource Center"

**Objective:** Capture top-of-funnel traffic (people researching *before* they buy).

### A. "Cost/Pricing Articles" (High Intent)
*   **Content:** "Custom Cabinet Cost Calculator for Houston"
    *   *Research Data (2025 Estimates):*
        *   **Stock (Big Box):** $150 - $300 per linear foot.
        *   **Semi-Custom:** $400 - $800 per linear foot.
        *   **Custom (YuDezign):** $800 - $1,500+ per linear foot depending on finish.[11][12][13]
    *   *Why this matters:* "Linear foot" is the industry standard metric. Publishing this transparency builds immense trust.
    *   *Differentiation:* Explain that "Stock" pricing often excludes installation, hardware, and crown molding, whereas Custom usually includes the full service.

### B. "Design Inspiration"
*   **Content:** "Trends for 2025"
    *   *Trend 1: Warm Minimalism.* Moving away from stark sterile white to "Taupe," "Mushroom," and "Natural White Oak".[14][3]
    *   *Trend 2: The "Appliance Garage".* Countertop cabinets with pocket doors to hide coffee makers/blenders.
    *   *Trend 3: Slab Backsplashes.* Matching the quartz countertop up the wall (requires precise cabinet installation).

### C. "How-To Guides"
*   **Content:** "How to Measure for Custom Cabinets"
    *   *Technical Tip:* Explain "Out of Square" walls. Most Houston walls are not perfectly straight. Custom cabinets can be "scribed" to fit the wall; stock cabinets require ugly filler strips.
    *   *Step-by-Step:* Measure width at three points (top, middle, bottom) and use the smallest number.[15][16][17]

## 5. Research for "Priority 5: Comparison Pages"

**Objective:** Win the "VS" keywords (high conversion intent).

### A. `/vs/frameless-vs-framed-cabinets`
| Feature | Framed (Traditional) | Frameless (European/YuDezign) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aesthetics** | Visible frame, traditional look | Sleek, tight gaps (1/8"), modern |
| **Storage** | Less (Frame blocks opening) | **10-15% More Volume**[5][9] |
| **Access** | Center stile often blocks access | Full access |
| **Installation** | Easier (frame hides error) | Requires expert precision (walls must be square) |

*   *SEO Angle:* Target the query "Do frameless cabinets have more storage?" (Answer: Yes).

### B. `/vs/melamine-vs-laminate-vs-acrylic`
*   **Melamine (TFL):**
    *   *Pros:* Cost-effective, consistent pattern, highly durable against scratches. Great for closet interiors and garage cabinets.
    *   *Cons:* Can chip at edges if not banded well.
*   **High-Pressure Laminate (HPL):**
    *   *Pros:* Impact resistant, widely available textures (wood grain, linen).
    *   *Cons:* Slightly more expensive than Melamine.
*   **Acrylic:**
    *   *Pros:* High gloss "glass-like" finish, UV stable (won't yellow). The premium choice for modern kitchens.
    *   *Cons:* Shows fingerprints, higher cost.[1][2]

## 6. Strategic Recommendations for Implementation

### 1. The "Local Evidence" Strategy
For every Location page, do not just write text. **Embed a Google Map** centered on that neighborhood and, if possible, mention a nearby landmark.
*   *Example for The Woodlands:* "Serving homes near The Woodlands Waterway and Carlton Woods."
*   *Why:* Google's Local algorithm looks for proximity signals.

### 2. The "Schema" Opportunity
Your strategy mentions `FAQPage` schema. Ensure you also use `Product` schema for the Service pages.
*   *Tip:* You can mark up "Kitchen Cabinet" as a product with a price range (`priceRange: "$$$"`). This can help display pricing tiers in search results.

### 3. Visual Content Requirements
For the "Comparison" pages, text is not enough.
*   **Action:** Create simple 2D graphics showing a "Top Down View" of a Framed vs. Frameless cabinet to visually prove the storage difference. This is "link bait"—other sites will link to your image if it's good.

### 4. Trust Signals
On the `/pricing` and `/process` pages, include a section on **"The 2025 Supply Chain."**
*   *Research:* Lead times have stabilized post-pandemic, but quality hardware (Blum/Hettich hinges) can still have delays. Stating "We stock our own hardware to prevent delays" (if true) is a massive selling point.[13]
